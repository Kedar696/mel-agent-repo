## 5. Survey quality: what we can prove today and what is still open
- **Proven:** decision 99.3% vs DG's answer key; format, safety, timeliness 100% on every survey; P4 relevance
  0.81; AI judge 4.9/5, and the judge agrees with people (within 1 point 97%, people with each other 83%).
- **Not yet proven: "Bedrock writes better surveys than the templates."** The judge gives Bedrock 4.9 and templates
  4.5, but Bedrock's surveys are about **twice as long** (136 vs 65 characters of question text in the new sheet),
  and a judge may just reward length. The fix is a blind side-by-side: `docs/results/blind_sheet.csv`, 20 pairs
  (today's writer vs templates, every advice type that gets a survey, A/B order random, key in
  `blind_sheet_key.json`). Each scorer fills a copy; `python scripts/blind_sheet.py score docs/results/blind_sheet_*.csv`
  gives mean scores, which one people picked, a sign test, and **whether the longer survey simply won**. It replaces
  the older 15-row `hand_scores_bedrock.csv` (an earlier writer, and no wait-and-watch or expert surveys).
- **Until it is scored, say on stage:** "The AI judge prefers the Bedrock surveys; we're checking with a blind
  human comparison because the judge may like longer questions." That is honest and shows method.

## 6. Scheduling: what we can prove
- Send window from the action window and crop calendar: timeliness 100%; brief-vs-answer-key trade-off shown with
  numbers.
- Order: by send-window start, ties by priority (`queue_key`).
- Weekly cap: **0 cap violations** on the full pack (1,613 sent, 425 moved, 480 merged, 98 skipped).
- Rejected surveys never take a farmer's weekly slot (test).
- Season walk to end of March: ~2,050 sends, 0 blocked, ~5 s; channel fallback waits per channel; opt-outs (STOP,
  /stop, 9) stop further sends; late answers counted (`schedule.late_answers`).
